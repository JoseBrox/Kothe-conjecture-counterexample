## Relationship of this manuscript to the formal proof

The mathematical construction in `paper.tex` is not an independent discovery. It is a human-readable reconstruction and simplification of the formal proof above.

The main reorganizations are expository rather than changes to the counterexample:

- the common-zero argument is presented through a Segre-Veronese embedding and the projective dimension theorem;
- the finite linearization of a noncommutative polynomial is written explicitly;
- the periodic rank-reduction construction is stated directly in conventional matrix language;
- the final passage to two nil left ideals is proved explicitly, so the conclusion does not rely on quoting Krempa's equivalence theorem.

The formal source uses an algebraic closure of `F_2`. The manuscript states the construction over an arbitrary countable algebraically closed field because the proof only uses countability and algebraic closedness at the corresponding steps.

## Verification status

The formal theorem is machine checked in the source repository. The manuscript here has been repeatedly audited for the projective dimension count, Cauchy-Binet degree estimate, rank reduction over `k(s)`, periodic construction, noncommutative linearization, weighted-shift composition order, companion-matrix eigenvector argument, and the final left-ideal calculation.

This distinction is important: machine checking of the original Lean theorem does not by itself constitute machine checking of this reorganized prose proof. Independent mathematical review of the manuscript remains valuable.
